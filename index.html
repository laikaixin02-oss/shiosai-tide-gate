How it’s made

Every ridge, roof tile, petal and koi is written into being at load time. There are no models or image textures — only height fields, signed distances and shaders.

The 400 × 400 terrain is baked once with a CPU horizon-AO pass. Water is a two-target pass: scene colour and depth are copied aside, then the surface reconstructs optical thickness, refraction, Beer–Lambert absorption and screen-space reflections. A 256 × 256 wave equation runs at a fixed 60 Hz.

Move the cursor over the water to send ripples across the bay. Change the tide and watch the koi find deeper water.
